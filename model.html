The Concept

What is
tokenization?

Tokenization is the process of representing ownership rights in a real asset — in this case, real estate — through a digital instrument. Each token is not a cryptocurrency: it is a legally documented participation in the economic rights of a specific property, backed by a defined legal structure.

Think of it as the digital equivalent of a share in a real estate fund — with the transparency, auditability, and efficiency that modern technology enables, but rooted in the same legal and regulatory frameworks as traditional asset management.
