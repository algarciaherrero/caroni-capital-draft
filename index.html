The Opportunity

Institutional real estate has always generated exceptional returns.
Access to it has not been equally distributed.

Premium hotels, prime retail assets, and landmark properties have historically been reserved for large funds and institutional investors. Caroni changes that — providing structured, regulated, and transparent access to the same asset class, for a broader pool of qualified investors.

Why it works

Technology as the guarantee,
not the product.

Tokenization is not a shortcut — it is the infrastructure that makes this investment more transparent, more auditable, and more enforceable than traditional alternatives.

Full Traceability

Every transaction is recorded and permanently auditable. No black boxes — full visibility for investors, regulators, and auditors at every stage.

Legal Structure First

Each asset sits inside a dedicated SPV with enforceable legal rights. The token represents a real legal interest — not a promise. Documented, structured, and legally binding.

Regulated Compliance

Structured to meet European digital asset frameworks. KYC and AML verification on every investor. Built for regulatory scrutiny — because that scrutiny protects everyone.

The same transparency that satisfies regulators is what protects investors. Caroni is structured from the ground up for compliance — designed to meet the standards of European digital asset frameworks and institutional investment regulation.

The Process

Three steps.
One framework.

01

Asset Selection

Caroni identifies and underwrites institutional-grade real estate assets — primarily urban hotels, boutique properties, and prime retail — applying rigorous due diligence standards across location, operator quality, and return profile.

02

Structuring & Tokenization

Each asset is placed into a dedicated legal vehicle (SPV). Ownership interests are then represented as digital tokens through Block Invest — our technology partner — providing full legal backing, transparency, and auditability.

03

Investor Access

Qualified investors access the opportunity through the Block Invest platform. Each investment carries economic participation rights, rental income exposure, and potential capital appreciation — all documented and enforceable.
